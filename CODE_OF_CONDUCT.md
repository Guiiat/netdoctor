# Code of Conduct

NetDoctor is built for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and points
of view. Be respectful, constructive, and patient in every project space.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal traits.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect privacy; never post another person's credentials or internal network details.
- Help make participation safe and welcoming, especially for newcomers.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and
other abusive behavior are not tolerated. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public
issue containing sensitive personal information. Maintainers will review reports fairly, preserve
confidentiality where possible, and avoid conflicts of interest.
